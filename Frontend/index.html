How to Play Ortenau Guesser

1

Random Drop in the Ortenau Region

Each round drops you into a random street-level location strictly inside the Ortenaukreis (Baden-Württemberg, Germany) — between the Rhine river in the west and the Black Forest crest in the east.

2

Fixed Point — Look Around 360° (No Moving)

Your position is fixed to the drop point. Drag to pan 360° horizontally and tilt vertically, or scroll to zoom in on road signs, half-timbered houses, vineyards, valley slopes, and compass directions.

3

Pin Your Guess on the Bottom-Right OpenStreetMap

Hover or expand the OpenStreetMap mini-map on the bottom right, click anywhere in the Ortenau region to place your guess pin, and click Lock In Guess.

4

See the Real Distance & Score

After submitting, the result map reveals the actual location, your guess marker, the connecting line, and the exact real-world distance (in meters and kilometers) calculated via OpenStreetMap geoservices.
